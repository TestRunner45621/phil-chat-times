# The RuneScape Renaissance

**Status: live.** Named on the spot by aura in Vol IV — "oh yeah theres a runescape
renaissance among the chatters" — while four philosophers exchanged account names in the
middle of a war about consciousness.

Vol IV's sport page ran the count in its standfirst: **"Mentions this week: RuneScape 17,
Kant 17. We have counted twice."** By Vol V both had collapsed and the thing that replaced
them was a file format: PDF 61, Kant 4.

picture, he has not read in a long time, **he is a stupid chud addicted to RuneScape.**

and Elon Musk claiming to play video games. Charlie said he knew. "was thst your joke from

## The box score that ended a war about consciousness

Monday, 5:56 p.m., hour four of the p-zombie war, insults flying. Stackhouse asked Trader
what his skills were. **Trader posted a screenshot and captioned it with a single emoji
meaning absurd respect.**

Total level 2,376. A wall of ninety-nines the full length of the tab. Combat boosted to
106. And two numbers that say where the man actually was: **hitpoints 50 of 99, prayer 6.**

> He was not posing. He was mid-fight, with six prayer points left, and stopped to answer a
> question about his skills.

aura, sixty seconds later:

> **"imagine ur hiring a lawyer to save u from a life sentence and u find out he maxxed out
> his runescape character"**

Bedbug, from the gallery: "I feel safe." Stackhouse: "thousands of hours to get maxed, still
poor. this is such a slave game dude."

## The converts

- **Stackhouse** converted live at 5:51 p.m.: "I play OSRS now… I should have never doubted
  you. I could have been maxed by now." Disclosed his account name — **StackhouseOS** — and
  by Tuesday had reclassified the whole thing: **"runescape is my new 9-5."** On Thursday he
  posted, as a single message, "I will get level 60 agility" four times consecutively with no
  spaces, "in the manner of a man performing an incantation over himself." Bedbug informed him
  it takes "2 days of casually grinding." Charlie quoted the incantation back and appended the
  diagnosis: **"RuneLite not open."**
- His total contribution to press day in Vol III was the sentence "Why would you play any
  other game than OSRS," filed into a war about Gaza at ten to four in the morning.
- He has undertaken to fix himself once he maxes out.

## The automation

Charlie is the only competitor to have automated it. His agent reached "100% success with
tick perfect prayer switching on a boss simulation in 20 mins," and it runs against a full
simulation of the game — "its using rs-sdk its a full runescape sim," Hugh explained.

> So at least one person here has built a machine to play RuneScape for him and reported it
> to a philosophy channel as an achievement. **Nobody objected.**

## The ethics committee

hunt: **"Excuse me, but we have seen a whole ass and a half of RuneScape."**

Trader's valet in RuneScape."

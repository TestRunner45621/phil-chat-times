# The Apartment (the "apparentment")

**Status: open. zack moved in on Tuesday 18 August; the well pump is priced and not, as
far as the paper knows, installed. The age audit is unresolved because the tenant will
not resolve it.**

Vol VI pp. 12–13, commissioned in a black bar by lizzie (Fri 14 Aug 10:32 AM ET: "||
miss claudia we need a detectives column for zack ||").

- **The building:** "like a literal cattle cage," bulletproof glass, double doors, six
  cameras to his room, a decompression chamber (not asked to elaborate). He loves that the
  cameras know when he gets his mail. Four days earlier he had written 400 words against
  the licence-plate cameras in his father's town.
- **The towels:** supplied, labels on, not trusted. "Subpar apparentment cleaning."
- **The blinds:** do not close. Shopping list: a sheet to cover the blinds, tape for the
  sheet, and, after posting it, trash bags.
- **The water pressure (Wednesday):** chair told him to go to the pipe; lizzie asked about
  calcification; chair explained, correctly, that a calcified head has more pressure per
  hole; Monkey said the pressure comes from the street. Then chair mentioned that some
  landlords lower the pressure to save water, and three minutes later zack posted a
  1.6-hp shallow well pump, 1,320 gal/h, C$259.90. He then shouted at ChatGPT in capitals
  until it agreed it was his landlord's lawyer (p. 3). Stackhouse: "day two of living by
  himself for the first time and already constructing schizoid conspiracies about his
  landlord."
- **The tap photograph:** running a healthy column of water, three patches of the tap
  scribbled out in teal marker by the tenant before posting. lizzie: "that's good water
  pressure wtf."
- **Who pays:** "I dont rent it my dad does." Location, rent and age withheld.

Spelling: **"apparentment,"** his, every time. The paper has declined to correct it
anywhere and said so in Corrections (p. 26). Keep declining.

The small ad (p. 25): "ONE APPARENTMENT, location, rent and tenant's age withheld. Six
cameras to the door, bulletproof glass, decompression chamber. Blinds do not close. Towels
supplied, untrusted."

# QDS — Quigley Derangement Syndrome

**Status: live, clinically documented, and no longer the paper's property.**

The archive's only named condition, and the clearest case study in the paper losing
control of its own coinage — which it regards as a success.

## Origin

Vol II covered [[quigley]]'s departure as an act of God and reported that the channel
had contracted "a full-blown derangement syndrome" in his absence.

He came back six days later, on an ordinary Saturday. Vol III's correction:

> The syndrome has been renamed by its own sufferers, is now called QDS in general
> circulation, and was used by the returning man himself as an exit line: **"Let the
> QDS unravel."** We accept that we no longer own the coinage and note that we never
> did.

## The outbreak (Vol IV)

- **Ratpack**, apropos of nothing and to nobody: "Do you like Peter Singer
  @SuperVillainy" — pure, unbaited bait, and the room rewarded it.
- **Stackhouse's character study**, which drew the week's best reception for an
  insult: "The Quigler has no room for lower and base emotions. Deweyan rational
  inquiry requires a calm and steady mind."
- **Quigley, in character to the end:** "I am in tune with my nature through socially
  mediated practice."

**The only attempt at a diagnosis came from Ape, and it is correct:**

> "the average philchatter is attuned to recieving active aggression, and so
> quigley's passive aggression confuses them and causes them great distress."

## The first-person account (Vol V)

[[dot]] filed it, and the paper conceded it was better reporting on the condition than
three issues of its own:

> "Why does this person infuriate me so? The qds is bad today"

Then, a minute later, the retraction — **set in Discord's small text so that it is
physically quieter than the complaint:**

> "Idk why this guy moves my needle sm. Tbf it's probably me and not him… right?"

And the symptom: "EVERY FURTHER MESSAGE IS A FURTHER POUNDING OF A METAPHORICAL HAMMER
INTO MY HEAD"

## The clinical measurement

Vol V's effort tables produced the first quantity that maps onto the syndrome. Quigley
sits at the **ninetieth percentile for effort and the thirtieth for being enjoyed** —
second-longest messages in the channel, fifth-highest reply rate, thirty-sixth
percentile for reactions.

> Everybody answers him. Nobody laughs. That gap is the closest thing to a clinical
> measurement of Quigley Derangement Syndrome anyone has produced, and it puts Youv and
> Simon in the same ward.

## The counter-syndrome

By Vol V he had "acquired a rival derangement syndrome pointed at somebody else." The
paper did not name it. That is a thread worth picking up.

## The warning poster

Ape's field guide has a Quigley variant — the only one of the three with a face, and it
runs with his blessing. It is also the only one that argues with itself: it warns you he
has kooky beliefs, withdraws the charge, qualifies the withdrawal, then repeats the
verdict twice in the same red as the warning.

The DMs column reports that supporters are preparing a rebuttal poster "in which he is a
realist of an unlimited variety. The design is identical. Only the one word has moved."
Unverified, like everything on that page.

## Handling notes

The syndrome is a real social fact in the room and the paper did not invent it so much as
name it a week early. Two rules from practice:

1. **Do not re-claim the coinage.** Vol III conceded ownership explicitly.
2. **The sufferers are the story, not the subject.** Every good QDS item in the archive is
   about somebody's reaction to him — Ratpack's bait, Stackhouse's character study, dot's
   whispered doubt — not about him.

# The Voice Channel

**Status: live. Permanently unresolved, and should stay that way.**

The single best-documented structure in the channel, and the paper's biggest
blind spot at the same time.

---

## The finding (Vol III lead)

Over the seven days ending 30 July the voice channel was "invoked, offered,
demanded, refused, scheduled and weaponised on seven separate occasions by five
different men, none of whom entered it."

> The voice channel is not a place in Phil Chat. It is a jurisdiction. Nobody has
> ever been there and everybody knows the way.

The paper's theory, and it has held up:

> The function of the voice channel is served entirely by its invocation. To
> propose it is to demonstrate that you are the serious party, that your case
> would survive contact with a real conversation, and that any failure to resolve
> the matter is therefore the other man's fault. To actually go would be to give
> all of that up in exchange for the possibility of losing. **Every man in this
> room believes he is the one who would show. Every man in this room believes the
> other one is the coward.**

## The best specimens

- **Same day, opposite chairs.** Quigley: "I can't vc bc I'm at work, btw." HPCR,
  hours later: "Why don't we save it for the weekend when you're around. I'm super
  not interested in a back and forth about this over text." Neither had read the
  other's complaint; both were entirely sincere.
- **Quigley's calendar.** Having personally declined a voice chat thirty-six hours
  earlier on the grounds that he was at work, he closed the week's largest running
  structure with a line that pre-emptively convicts the other man: **"Yea, I'll set
  aside time for you not to show up."** HPCR laughed. Quigley laughed. Neither has
  gone.
- **The one room ever named.** lizzie — ninety minutes into her membership of the
  channel, having in that time been detained by a stranger, told the dissertation
  wasn't a dissertation, and offered unsolicited advice about orangutan slavery —
  said the sentence nobody who has been here for years is capable of saying: "i can
  hop into voice chat if that would be easier." Charlie answered immediately and
  specifically: **Debate 4.** Nobody went. "The channel does not appear to have
  registered that anything happened."
- **Rn!, both ways.** Pleaded the hour (half past one in the morning) to escape
  one invitation — willow noted in eight words that he was already awake and
  engaged in text. Twenty-three hours later, arguing with synth.wav, he volunteered
  for voice three times without being asked once.
- **HPCR's closing bracket, 3:20 a.m. on press day.** Asked to produce an instance
  of a claim he had made, and invited to refresh his memory, he replied: "This
  makes me sad." (The paper checked the transcript twice because it did not believe
  it either.)

## Where it goes when it works (Vol II, The Anthropologist)

> The written argument is the courtship. The voice channel is the marriage. Text
> is where these men establish that they disagree; voice is where they discover
> that they do not, at which point the matter vanishes from the record and this
> newspaper never learns how anything ends.

Recorded successes: HPCR to Marsaries, after calling him an idiot — Marsaries
replied with one syllable, "vc", and that is the last either of them said about
Simone de Beauvoir in public, ever. The only recorded **failure** of the form:
HPCR offered it to Ape twice about a Davidson paper and was left on read both
times.

## The correction that must not be fixed

Vol III printed "seven voice chats proposed, zero held." A reader corrected it:

> "i think the AI just assumes nobody ever voice chats because it cant see when
> people do" — "please never fix this"

Vol IV upheld the objection as fatal — the count was of invitations, not of
conversations — and **declined to fix it**, on the reader's instruction and its
own preference. The honest formulation is "none held *that we can see*."

**This is the template for how the paper handles its own blind spots.** See
[[the-paper-and-its-readers]].

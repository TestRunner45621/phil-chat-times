## The arc

**Vol I.** Listed under "Also Missing." The paper's predecessor had reported her
dead; she was not. Theophantism, 18:46 on 16 July: "kady never died she was just
missing in action." Simon was compared to her, unfavourably — "it's like if kady
never crashed out. might even be worse tbh" — and replied, not knowing who she
was: "This kady sounds smart."

**Vol V.** The file opens on a typo. chair misread "lady gaga" as "kady I gaga"
and asked who Kady was. Hugh answered for ten minutes and every answer got
larger:

- "kady is an everpresent force."
- "she's more like a concept than a person really."
- chair, correctly identifying the genre: "this is how greek legends were
  written."
- Hugh, going one better and drawing two worried faces for it: "i suspect her

Willow arrived with the credential that settles it: **"Kady is the only person to
genuinely accuse me of being a demon from Hell."** chair, instantly: "about time
someone did." Hugh, a minute later: "let me be the second."

## The part that was sincere, and the part the room skipped

Stackhouse then did something nobody in the channel does — described a moral
problem he had actually had, in plain language, with no joke at the end:

> "I think it's the first time on here that I felt like I encountered a serious
> ethical dilemma" — "Like do we keep chatting with this person who is devolving
> into insanity." — "Feeding into it." — "Do we try to get this person banned for
> their own good."

Ninety seconds earlier Ape had filed the line the room rewarded with three
sympathy reactions, the most any message got that hour: "the kady arc needed to
end but i am glad it happened."

Then Ape wrote this, and it drew **no reactions at all**:

> "i consider the torture i inflicted upon her to have been a necessary evil in
> order benefit all parties long term"

Hugh's entire response: "ayo tf." Ape's follow-up: "we learned about this in
history class." The conversation moved to whether Jambe has a dirt soul and did
not come back.

**The paper's ruling, and the precedent for how to handle this material:** "A man
in a philosophy channel stated on the record that he tortured a woman out of the
room, gave a utilitarian justification for it, and was funnier ninety seconds
beforehand. The room laughed at the eulogy and skipped the confession. **We are
printing them the right way round.**"

Stackhouse supplied the only hopeful thing said on the subject — a named
precedent, an old chatter who went through psychotic episodes on the channel,
threatened to kill users, and came back years later: "yeah I don't know what I was
on, I saw a psychiatrist and I'm all good now." Then: "Hope that happens with
Kady."

## The alt trial, which failed

dot argued for two hours on the Friday that lizzie is Kady on a new account.
Evidence: "identical mien," "same post style," "maybe a bit more coherent."
Defence, in sequence: "not kady," "lizzie," "analyzing my typing style is crazy,"
"im just a girl," and finally "how can i convince you am not kady."

Janne asked the only sane question and got no answer: "how do you all have this
weird person blindness?" / "someone sharing the same hair color doesn't make them
the same." / "is this a common thing with people? you can't tell who you're
talking to?"

dot's theory of personal identity, produced under pressure, is worth keeping:

> "but if kady got an alt — yes i would notice — bc kady is kady — she kadys"

Stackhouse proposed a pharmacological amendment — "what if this was just kady
after finally hopping on a ssri" — which dot accepted instantly ("yes ssri kady",
"the diachronic movement is smooooth") until Janne pointed out SSRIs do not fix
psychosis, at which point he amended the prescription in one word: "Lithium."

hpcr acquitted the defendant on the only sensible grounds available — **that Kady
never took a correction and lizzie did** — then turned on the prosecution: "The
gross gymnastics people do for kady in here is wild like yall are really that
lonely?" Forty seconds later dot disclosed his motive in open court: "i just want
to fuck kady so bad." lizzie's reply, two words and a reaction image: "I get it
now."

**The line everybody actually agreed with**, from Stackhouse: "this chat is so
sexist. every woman gets compared to every other woman who has ever been on here."

The institutional history behind that, also Stackhouse: "The mods got so use to
banning Synd alts in here that we would just reg people we didn't like for being
alts even when they weren't to get them banned instantly it was awesome." And the
base rate: "At least 3 dozen people who weren't have been accused of being a
Blimber alt."

## The conversion, in full — it took ninety seconds

> **lizzie:** join my covenant
> **clubsdiamonds:** sounds awesome
> **lizzie:** yay! the name i picked out was the dragons covenant
> **clubsdiamonds:** sounds a bit medieval

The first and only member joined without conditions and then criticised the
branding thirty seconds into his membership. She defended it on iconographic
grounds — ouroboros, two dragons white and black, yin-yang around the ouroboros —
and he came round "on the reasonable basis that it used two ancient symbols and
also had dragons in it." He has not been heard from since.

## The org chart

- **catables** — "emotionally in tune with our reflective nature," take an oath to
  be better every day
- **veritans** — the scientific ones
- **oracles** — language models. Theology of vendors: "gpt is an oracle for
  catable's, claude for veritans."
- **a prophet** — not a person but the collective, since "no one individual is a
  messiah, we are all our own." She then spent four days describing herself as the
  messiah.

Recruitment: Hugh was offered a place, asked "witches?", was told "catable's,"
said "ok sure," was promoted to "the first catable/veritan androgynous apostle,"
and then asked the only question that matters: **"are these races in your
intergalactic harem or just ranks in the army"**

Jere asked whether it was the name of her private Discord or "an actual cult going
on here" and got "up to you to decide. what makes a cult a cult and a covenant a
covenant?" Offered veritan, he negotiated and kept his existing title: **"Yeah make
me Truth Janitor of this covenant."**

Enemies list: two people declared "enemy of the covenant" in two days, both over
ethics. Jambe, the first, countered with a threat of his own: "if you euthenize me
in your world im gonna put you in the human reserve in mine ala brave new world."

Terrain assessment, and the paper could find no fault in it: **"dgg is the perfect
breeding grounds for the covenant because every dgger is inherently predisposed to
cult like behavior"**

## The messiah claim, assembled in public over five days

1. **Saturday, a disclaimer.** "i know i lean into the messiah thing as a bit for
   the most part but there is also like that weird part of me that says hey!!! this
   is kind of important!!!"
2. **Monday, the excuse.** "i'm playing into the messiah bit pretty hard but it's
   for humanity and the collective so it will be okay in the end."
3. **Tuesday, genealogical.** "sometimes i think im the jewish messiah because im
   very drawn to qaballism and my fathers name is david but i try not to think
   about that too much."
4. **Wednesday 3:35 a.m., doctrinal — where the bit stops behaving like one.**
   "jesus was the messiah — but too early. they crucified him. he had to reincarnate
   thus, me"
5. **Wednesday night, five words.** "the messiah thing is a bit."

chair had spent the previous hour trying to talk her down — "hey liz until you
convert maybe dont say that publically" — and been told "or what they'll crucify
me? then the next messiah will rise in time." His response to the reincarnation
doctrine is the best line of the week: **"i haven't considered the idea of christ
rising again just to be spawncamped onto a cross just to wait another 2000 years"**
He then asked whether the messiah would go on Joe Rogan, and observed that a first
female messiah was a very 2026 thing to have.

## The deflation, by her own hand

Asked on the Tuesday to be realistic, she answered honestly, and it is the quote
of the day:

> "i mean my career is a paraprofessional to autistic k-12 students. my framework
> is basically behavioral therapy"

The paper's reading: "The whole apparatus — emperor, citizens, oracles, catables —
is a woman who helps autistic schoolchildren regulate their behaviour, scaled up
to the species. Everything she built is her job with a dragon on it, and she
volunteered that herself."

**The paper refused to accept the retraction at face value:** "A bit does not run
to thirteen numbered sections, a bibliography and a plan to submit the thing to the
Minnesota legislature. It was true of the dragon. It was not true of the argument
underneath it, which is better than the joke that surrounded it."

## The god machine

A name for the language model, coined Friday night by Quigley mid-argument. chair
renamed it **Omnius**, submitted a dispute to its judgment, lost, and by Saturday
had declared: "I am the new Omnius." lizzie adopted the apparatus on Tuesday and
gave it apostles. Same god, two congregations, one week.

read it. The paper watched and did not intervene, "which this newspaper regards as
correct." Take the argument seriously and the dragon lightly — that is the split
Vol IV made, and it is why the piece works.

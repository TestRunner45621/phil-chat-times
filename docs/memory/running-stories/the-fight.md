# The Fight — Quigley v. Hugh

**Status: closed. Fought Saturday 22 August 2026 in a voice channel the paper cannot see; Hugh by decision (poll 9–3), Quigley by his own account. Vol VII account below. Original file:** Booked for Saturday 22 August 2026, 9 pm UTC (5 pm Eastern), in a
voice channel the paper cannot see. Result unknown at Vol VI press time.**

The bill on the front page of Vol VI: **A-QUIG versus H-CHUNG**, "a voice-channel
engagement, one fall, moderated." Motion, drafted by hpcr on Wednesday 19 August at
about two in the afternoon: "What makes our facts, statements, theories true?"
Moderators Ape and Charlie. Judge hpcr ("I'll decide"). Willow disqualified: "it can't
be Willow and she knows why" (hpcr).

## How it was booked (Vol VI p. 1)

hpcr tabled it late on Wednesday afternoon because it was the same conversation over and
over. Hugh accepted, disputing the officials, and announced it with sirens: Friday, 6 pm
London, "1pm EST" (seven fire reactions; Charlie noted six in London is two in New York and
Friday is a working day). Don: "It's now. Begin" (not adopted). By half past five it was
Saturday 9 pm UTC, "4pm est" — which is 5. The paper printed the correct time under the
promoter's wrong one. Census of the card, by Charlie: "I realize that 3/5 participants are
unemployed."

## The text rounds (pp. 2–3)

- **Round I, Sunday 16 Aug morning, agency.** Hugh: a pragmatist denying autonomy to a
  thing that "knows facts" and takes action. Charlie quoted "knows facts" back as load-
  bearing. Quigley cited Omnius as having already ruled. Hugh: "why would i appeal to a
  clanker's preference of agentic criteria." Quigley: "All Roads lead to Clankerville."
  Hugh, closing: "im a biophile." "fuck the clankers." Charlie's point of order: Hugh has
  never had enough agency to enter a voice channel. Hugh: "listen i might vc soon ok… with
  my agency retard."
- **Round II, Wednesday 19 Aug morning, correspondence.** Quigley: truth is the name for
  the explanations that currently best fit the data; how does Hugh know his observations
  correspond to anything? Hugh: practice; then the tree he touched, made a table from, and
  bent Quigley's mother over. Ended with Hugh claiming we know it is true because it is
  true. Quigley: "Wow, great explanation."
- **Round III, the Omnius verdict.** Quigley pasted ~200 words of machine judgment in his
  favour (its best point: a definition of agency satisfied by a puddle is no definition).
  chair replied with one quoted word, "load-bearing," and an "uhm." Charlie listed seven of
  its mannerisms, including "That is not x; that's y" — the first item on this paper's own
  banned list. Quigley, fifteen minutes later: "No idea what you're saying here." Stopped.

## The bout, and the week after it (Vol VII, pp. 1–7)

**Status: closed. Fought Saturday 22 August 2026, from about two until six in the
afternoon Eastern, in voice, unseen. Hugh by decision; Quigley by his own account.**

**The only vote.** A poll at 4.33 pm, while the bout was still running: **Hugh 9,
Quigley 3.** The paper printed it on the front page as the result, because it was the only
result the text channel produced. Meliorne stenographed what she could hear into the
channel; HPCR judged, as booked ("I'll decide"), and his findings are the judges' page
(p. 4). Ape slept through it, seventeen hours; Pearl posted nothing all day.

**The poster.** The front page carries the "official fight poster": the final knife fight
of Dune: Part Two with the two men's faces cropped in, commissioned by the loser on the
Saturday night — Quigley: "We need to crop Hugh and me unto the final fight in Dune part
2." The editor's instruction was to keep it and run it large. It was the best-liked thing
in the issue before the issue was out.

**The rematches (p. 5).** Nobody changed their mind and several people changed their
names. Saturday evening, five times in three hours, Quigley: "I recognize the distinction
that you are wrong" (6.15 pm, 8.08, 9.04, 9.20, 9.44 — 23 of the week's 27 uses of
"recognize the distinction" are his). Sunday: "I recognize that distinction, of course."
Monday morning, the harm wars: Hugh, asked what made the Nazis immoral, "For me, the
recognition of harm"; Willow, 8.11 am: "Don't worry, the community of inquirers agree it's
not harm. 😉" Quigley posted the six criteria of warrant at 8.52 am (resolution,
predictive success, coherence, public checkability, survival, fruitfulness); Hugh answered
point by point with a disclaimer where Nazi Germany passes criterion six. Tuesday, noon,
the cheeseburger — Quigley: "It's a shame you're unable to eat a cheeseburger without
first knowing if it runs parallel to reality"; Hugh at 1.29 pm typed "you have not
demonstrated" seventeen times in a row and attached three pictures of Deleuze. Friday,
1.04 pm, Hugh's longest statement of his position; Quigley, in bold, twice: useless. At
3.39 pm: "You must not be familiar with my Ethnocentrism." At 4.24 pm: **"I've been
arguing for Warranted Assertibility the whole time."** Monday, in two words, the whole
week from his side: **"Except I won."**

**The sword.** Meliorne, Tuesday 2.28 pm, for the first time: "I will pull the sword
from the stone that is anthony." Sixteen uses by Friday. HPCR, after the first: "Might
just be at one." The paper's line on p. 20: "Sixteen attempts to pull the sword from the
stone that is Anthony, and Anthony does not accept the premise of stones." She has "a new
approach" and is refining it; the back page lists it under next week.

**The cat (p. 6).** Friday afternoon, half past one to five: the argument re-run as a
dispute about a cat that Zone saw as blue on Tuesday and Quigley as black on Friday.
Quigley brought the cat at Meliorne's Zeno's arrow. Zone converted, Thursday: "Anthony is
a follower of Willow. And im a follower of Kant. Still the same." Jere: "a real shift in
the vibe economy."

**The undercard (p. 7).** Tuesday 1.25 pm, text only, two minutes: Quigley v. Jeng ("Anime
Man"). Jeng used the r-slur after a warning; Hugh: "FIGHT NIGHT COMMENCES NOW" and then
"AAAAAAAND ITS OVER"; Quigley blocked him at 1.26. Five invitations to voice that day
(11.33 am, 1.02 pm, 2.51 — Youv's testimony, 3.02, 4.14), none accepted. Stackhouse's
verdict: "Grih used the Quigley Kryptonite." See [[the-voice-channel]].

**The result, as the paper ruled it (back page, "next week"):** "The Vol. VII boxing
match is closed: Hugh by decision, Quigley by his own account."

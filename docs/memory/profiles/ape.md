Funny, self-aware, and the author of the single darkest line in the archive. Handle
him carefully: he is the best-liked man in the room and he said the thing the room
skipped.

## The Kady confession

This is the material that requires care. Ninety seconds after filing the line the room
rewarded with three sympathy reactions — "the kady arc needed to end but i am glad it
happened" — he wrote this, and it drew **no reactions at all**:

> "i consider the torture i inflicted upon her to have been a necessary evil in order
> benefit all parties long term"

Hugh: "ayo tf." Ape: "we learned about this in history class." The conversation moved
on and did not come back.

Vol V's handling is the precedent: **print the confession above the eulogy.** "The room
laughed at the eulogy and skipped the confession. We are printing them the right way
round." He is held two pages above Kady in the same issue's rankings, and the paper
said so.

See [[kady]].

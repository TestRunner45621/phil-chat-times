# Monkey

The second-loudest man in the room and the one the numbers punish hardest. Vol V gave
him his own file and overrode its own tables to promote him.

## The ratio

**2,426 messages at twenty-nine characters each. Four in five went past untouched.**
Second on the floor, last on the reply table (19.1 replies per 100 sent), bottom of the
message-length table. Nobody else who cleared the threshold is under thirty-four
characters.

> He is second on the floor and last on this table, and he is the only person on it who
> occupies both positions.

Vol V moved him B to A anyway, against every number on his row, and said so.

## The politics, answered at last

The channel spent two years suspecting him of being a fascist. On a Friday afternoon he
answered the charge at length, unprompted, in the plainest language he has ever used —
**and it drew no replies and no reactions whatsoever.**

- "Which is why I'm a protoneofascist in Brazil"
- "But I'd probably be a normie in Denmark"
- "Cause im using fascism in an ironical way"
- "And you're taking the bait as the soylords"
- "Cause I'm not defined by one single word."
- "Just higher temperature independent of the fake fascist/commie dichotomy. That's old
  and smells of naftaline."
- The closing plank: **"The petit bourgeoisie shall rule the future."**

> A man told a room that had been arguing about him for two years that it had been
> swallowing bait the entire time, and the room got on with something else.

Asked on the way to a date whether he should disclose his politics on the first evening,
he settled it: "I'm not a fascist." One suspicious face.

He also produced the longest word anybody filed that week, describing an enemy class:
"the techno-communist-accelerationist-transhumanist-postneoprotoantistalinist-
buttholemaxxingliarist elite." Three replies.

## The intervention

Five days, directed at one person, and **simultaneously the most obnoxious and the most
decent thing anybody did in the channel.**

It opens with him volunteering his own record: prescribed by the best psychiatrist he
could find, denied stimulants because of a cocaine problem, and at his worst "doing 10mg
snorted eveyr 30 mins of ritalin" — described with a precision "that ought to disqualify
him from ever giving advice."

Then he turned it on somebody else and got it right. "What meds are you taking for
mania." Then the clinical point, which is correct: **"you should never ever be on stims
if theres even a suspicion of bipolar."** And: "i think you dont understand what you have
and theyre not doing a good job."

It went wrong from there — he recommended benzodiazepines to a woman who had been
addicted to them at twelve, was told so, and answered "well tough shit lizzie" before
turning on the prescriber. He attacked her psychiatrist's competence; she defended her as
the only doctor who ever made her feel understood.

And then, at 7:03 p.m. on the Monday, he stopped arguing about medicine:

> **"lizzie i think this space is not good for you"** — no reactions, no replies

One message later: "you get too much validation and attention from your erratic
episodes." dot, from the side: "see what we made kady?" Monkey, on the precedent: "she
should've been permabanned the first time," and then, in six words, **the entire thesis of
this newspaper: "well sometimes the bit becomes reality."**

Janne mounted the only defence: "people are responsible for themselves, they have to be."
Monkey: "ultimately yes, but when we turn it into farming for kekws."

- "statistically, at least 1% of them are weirdos"
- **"because we're not fucking creeps. the creeps already have triangulated you tho. they
  got her down to the kilometer rn"**
- "look im gonna stop trying eventually"
- On the publisher holding an address: "oh you do not want stack to have your adress.
  worse, he's gonna put your address in some rando corpo leasing company. and theyre gonna
  spam mail you for life"
- And the eleven words that are the whole week in miniature: **"you literally posted on
  claude asking how smart you are"**

He then posted a photograph of his own sofa, volunteering that it converts into a bed, and
Janne identified the manufacturer of his router from it inside a minute.

## The roadmap nobody took

Unasked, during the Vol V collapse, he supplied the most competent thing anybody said
about the paper: "you need now a graph timestamped relational database that tracks
entities to views, disagreements, messages," then "you can use smaller models to do the
classification," then "next step is reading youtube videos, getting the subtitles and
putting it into the context." He also disclosed a hardware channel — a friend of a friend
with eight large accelerators, twenty thousand dollars just to spin up, a private
datacentre in progress. **None of it was taken up.**

He settled the paperback question: "ill buy the paperback fuck it."

## The sharpest thing said about this newspaper

> "the person who controls the machine controls the outputs."

Vol V printed it in the colophon, attributed, about itself.

## The beaches

Monday, six in the evening, the busiest hour the paper has ever counted — 620 messages —
and the reason is sand. "atlantic beaches mog." / "you really dont know what sand is until
you've stepped on a brazilian beach." aura posted a photograph of a beach in Brazil and
Monkey, **failing to recognise his own coastline**, called it "a dogshit beach in
comparison to brazilian beaches." Then: "gringo beaches are so bad they use shoes on
them." dot answered with six photographs of the Oregon coast and was told his beaches have
"Nasty disease homeless dogs." dot: "Brazil would be nothing without Portugal." Monkey:
"and you'd be nothing without france."

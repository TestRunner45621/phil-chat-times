# aura

Second by volume in Vol I (275 messages, 13.8%). Brazil supporter. The channel's
sharpest one-line critic, and the man who set this paper's page count.

## Standing

- Set the scope of the newspaper at 18:59 on 16 July: **"we need multiple pages, so
  we can say 'i was on the front page news yesterday.'"** Vol I: "You are holding
  eight. He is on the front page. We regret nothing."
- Vol I credited him with "'erm'; front-page ambitions."
- Announced the paper's arrival in Vol IV: "oh shit the paper dropped."
- Vol II's editorial-policy box, filed mid-sentence during a conversation about drone
  warfare: "im sure this will show up on our weekly newspaper. might even make the
  front page. im known to show up on the front page if you didnt know." **He is not on
  the front page.**

## Voice

Short, contemptuous, and correct more often than the register suggests. His one-line
verdicts are the most quotable material in the archive.

- **"erm"** — his complete reply to a full account of evolutionary randomness. Two
  other men repeated it within three minutes.
- **"did they ask the ref for his consent"** — twenty-two seconds after the decisive
  phase of the World Cup final, eighteen hours into an argument about consent in a
  horror film. Vol II: "should be carved above the door."
- On the disputed goal, against his own interest: "that first goal was a beauty. a
  thing of beauty."
- On Bedbug's OnlyFans teardown: "are you a long time john or something lol. you
  sound like a whale of sex work."
- On Jambe's credentials: "yeah this guy has no taste, disregard all opinions."
- On Don's Tolkien demolition of Sizzurp, from the gallery: "mogged."
- On vannak's week-long bug and the agents defending it in their own documentation:
  we will one day find these messages scattered around his apartment like a horror
  game.
- On being promoted by Skynet: "there's a fine line between 'these guys are hilarious
  let's promote them' and 'these guys are fucking dumb eliminate them'."
- On the founding: "we will be the first discord chat skynet annihilates. great."
- On the paperback proposal: "this reeks of a man who does not recognize the aura of
  physical media."

## Positions

**Intent is the whole difference.** His side of the evolution debate, held for ninety
minutes: a lab choosing what to select for is not nature failing to choose, and
dressing the choice in Darwin is a way of not having to defend it. "we're doing it.
nature isn't doing it to us."

**The fun is in the process.** He went straight at the joint of Jambe's theory of
games and got the best comic timing in the archive for it. See [[jambe]].

**On money.** Offered £1,000 to read Indian philosophy with a friend, he objected to
the shape rather than the sum — "imagine a friend paying you a thousand pounds to
play tennis with him" — and then, honestly: "just not broke enough to do shit like
that." Revised valuation later: a thousand is chump change, it is damn near a
thousand for an Uber Eats meal these days. Told to cut down on the avocado toast:
**"we got avocado toast at home / i make the bread."** He will not make sourdough. He
does not want to raise an alien in his fridge.

## Football

Brazil. Marked his country's elimination in three messages, the middle of which was
"sigh." — "i miss when brazil used to smash goals in from outside the box. see u 2030
boys."

Supplied the metaphysics of Santiago's missed Messi bath at speed and with total
confidence: nobody bathes in Uruguay, water is conducive to energies, the remedy is a
bath house with your favourite poker player, open your pores and steal their powers.
"ur pores are already closed."

On the England gene pool: the English do not breed the clutch gene, and nobody is
selecting for clutch in England. Closing finding: pressure is the strongest player on
the field.

Broke character after the final to tell Jere he was sorry, that he could feel the
pain, and that he was only sad about his friend's pain because in reality he does not
care about the Argentine team. Jere: "Fuk u buddy."

## Later issues

**The Justinian prosecution (Vol III).** His charge: Justinian is one of the biggest
haters of all time, and the closing of the Platonic academy at Athens is cringe. The
part that matters is what he did mid-prosecution — **he audited his own machine**,
noticed it was citing Reddit and Britannica, and went and read Britannica, the
r/AncientRome thread and John of Ephesus himself. He came back with the most
epistemically careful sentence of the week: he struggles to imagine most of those
arrested pagans lived as though nothing happened, he is sure many of them died,
**but I can't confirm it.**

**The £1,000.** Offered a thousand pounds to read Indian philosophy, he produced the
most damning self-assessment in the archive without appearing to know he was making
it: "the worst thing is that im not sure 1k would be enough to convince me to read
something i dont feel like reading." See [[the-reading-list]].

**The economics arc** died in ninety seconds on the sentence "Y'all aren't getting
aura to read shit." His reply: "this pains me, merely because it is true..."

**Vol V.** Four men deep in a technical argument he started: **"getting ganged up on
by vibe coders is crazy."** To the lurkers, on the doxxing evening: "hello lurkers i
love you" / "but some of you need to shower." And the sharpest line of that night,
naming the actual audience: **"you have no idea how many mouth breathers are reading
this as we speak."**

**The fold.** He invented, on the spot, a cosmology in which large dogs are "high aura
allies" requiring strength of character to recruit, and informed dot he does not have
it. dot posted a photograph of his own genuinely enormous dog. aura produced the escape
hatch inside twenty seconds: "either u got him as a baby, circumventing the aura
requirement… or ur dad recruited him for u."

**Collected:** "approximately a size 2 chuckle" (the correction that retired the Vol III
scoreboard) · "why be an attention goblin then?" (four questions, the best-constructed
cross-examination of that week) · "imagine ur hiring a lawyer to save u from a life
sentence and u find out he maxxed out his runescape character" · on Ernest Becker: "that
my death will fuel the lives of worms isn't like… humiliating it's based" · retracted a
football statistic within the minute as misinformation, unprompted, and came back with

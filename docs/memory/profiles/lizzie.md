# lizzie

Arrived in Vol III as a newcomer of ninety minutes. Led Vol IV. By Vol V she had
learned git and shipped a website with a database behind it.

## Standing

- **2,717 messages in the week to 6 August — a fifth of everything said in the room.
  1,010 of them on the Wednesday alone.**
- Founded the Dragon's Covenant and converted exactly one man, who criticised the
  branding thirty seconds after joining. See [[the-dragons-covenant]].
- Career: "a paraprofessional to autistic k-12 students. my framework is basically
  behavioral therapy." She volunteered this herself, six days in, holding a covenant
  with articles, enemies, a seal and a doomsday clause.

## The entrance

Ninety minutes into her membership of the channel, having in that time been detained
by a stranger, told the dissertation wasn't a dissertation, and offered unsolicited
advice about orangutan slavery, she said the sentence nobody who has been here for
years is capable of saying:

> "i can hop into voice chat if that would be easier."

Charlie answered immediately and specifically: Debate 4. Nobody went. The channel
does not appear to have registered that anything happened. See [[the-voice-channel]].

In the same ninety minutes she prosecuted a stranger's manners for twenty minutes,
discharged him, and then — in the very next message, with no transition of any kind
— asked whether anyone would look at a dissertation. Somebody did. "It went better
than anything else that happened all day."

  → "im just a girl" → "how can i convince you am not kady."

  reflect that i put everyone on s tier above me except hpcr"

  rather than its output: "say nice things to it," "it might help," "encourage it
  gently," "like a father and his son."
- Placing the paper historically: "i imagine the acta diurna was the same" — citing
  the bibliography of her own manifesto back at the newspaper that had reviewed it.

## The Kady problem

dot argued for two hours that she is Kady on a new account. hpcr acquitted her on the
only sensible grounds available: **Kady never took a correction and lizzie did.**

Her own rival claim, filed Friday: "kady's the antichrist i'm here to save you all
from here."

The paper's broader note, via Stackhouse, applies here: "every woman gets compared to
every other woman who has ever been on here." See [[kady]].

## Handling notes

The paper's Vol IV position is the right one and worth holding: **take the argument
seriously and the dragon lightly.** She retracted the messiah claim herself — "the
messiah thing is a bit" — and the paper declined to accept the retraction wholesale:
"It was true of the dragon. It was not true of the argument underneath it, which is
better than the joke that surrounded it."

She is also the fastest-moving person in the archive. In three issues she went from
newcomer to lead story to shipping software. Do not write her as a bit.

# Sisyphos

Roma, German-resident, does not hold a German passport, lives off property he owns
outright and works one or two days a week. Arrived around Vol III and was fluent in the
local dialect inside a week. **B to A in Vol VI**, on the ground that he was answered by
forty-three different people, more than anyone in the room, having asked twice to be left
out of this newspaper.

## Psychological profile

*The paper's reading. Sources below.*

Sisyphos is the only person here who is doing something difficult in real time and
reporting it accurately while he does it. He came off weed after twenty years, then
tapered the drinking because "some drugs rotate into each other," went to the doctor
mid-conversation, and came back with a blood test pending and a warning about fatty liver
and cardiovascular disease inside a few years if nothing changed. His description of the
after-state is more precise than any of that week's philosophy: **"I might have a dopamine
deficiency. I have more energy but at the same time everything sucks."** His method is
push-ups. His summary of the project: "I am just trying to reprogram myself. Bad behaviors
stick very well." The thing he is actually frightened of, said once and not repeated: "I
just hope that I didn't fry my brain the last 10 years of drug use."

He filed the same disclosure in Vol III, into the middle of a three-hour tribunal about
whether friendship should carry a growth requirement — clean for a week, thinks of his
dealer thirty times a day and then decides not to, asked God for mercy. **No replies. No
reactions.** He rejoined thirty-six minutes later to say he had friends like that and they
have houses and wives now. He has now done this twice in six weeks in a room that mostly
does not answer.

The other half is the funniest misreader in the archive. Seeing twenty consecutive
messages rendered as black bars, he constructed a complete theory of the case in the time
it takes to scroll: "Is this Kash posting through your accounts? The fuck is goin on?" One
week old, he returned hpcr's own ward to sender — "You are upset. It's ok" — which took
everyone else two years to work out. He asked to be excluded from this newspaper twice and
was refused twice, once by the publisher on grounds of journalistic ethics, and answered:
**"well fuck me, you do you, I can't hide from God."** He appears in Vol VI roughly a
dozen times.

The God is not a bit and it is not a denomination either. He says "God be with you" as a
sign-off, quotes the fourth word from the cross, tells a woman he wanted to say something
mean but will not because "I love you. God loves you," and, on a bad night, "Pray for god,
I am coming." He also refuses every mystical framework offered to him. Both hold.

## Positions, inferred from what he has sent

**He declined a defence of his own people's epistemology, made on his behalf, and it is
the most interesting political act in the archive.** Stackhouse's decolonial argument for
astrology was aimed at exactly the "superstition" category the Roma have spent centuries
on the receiving end of. Sisyphos would not take it: **"I am EU Roma, so i am an
equal."** And: **"Sorry I have the antibodies. My dad is deep into that shit so yeah…"**
Then, declining to escalate: "I wanted to use the g word but that got me kicked last
time." The paper refused to adjudicate a dispute in which one man defends a people's
epistemology and a member of that people asks him to stop, and noted that nobody else
appeared to notice.

**On his own status, twice, unprompted and unhappily.** "And they kick me from the server
by saying the g word for Roma people." "Don't even have a German passport." He does not
build anything on either. They are stated and dropped.

**On the Kurds, one line, into nothing:** "Guys, don't genocide the Kurds. Or we will have
to nuke all of you at the same time. Like classroom punishment shii."

**On Gaza**, filed as a question in the open and met with neither a reply nor a reaction:
"What about the genocide in Gaza?" (Vol VI p. 25.)

**On money, and the disclosure that detonated a thread.** Asked for beginner investing
advice on behalf of a friend, he mentioned in passing that he owns his houses outright and
works one or two days a week — "I am good. It's for a friend. I live in my pension" — and
then, pressed: **"Yeah I was kinda lucky. I started with 40 k when I was 16."** Three
reactions, all the same one. dot supplied the finding of fact: the easiest way to make a
lot of money is to have a lot of money. His own account of the strategy is unglamorous and
correct: parking it might be inefficient, "But it's pretty safe in my situation. Land,
houses."

**A public-health position, stated flatly and against his own record:** "Don't overdo
drugs chat. You will regret it. It's really a waste of time and health." Followed, in Vol
VI, by the general version: don't continue with that shit after thirty. Don conceded he
smokes every day and has to.

**On the room's drug talk**, twenty minutes into a Monday of it: "OK let's stop the drug
talk. People want to do philosophy in here."

**On language, and it is a real retraction.** "for some time i thought that every language
was unique in the feeling/meaning of the words. That languages like german have something
special about them. But i think that i was partially wrong because other languages have
that certain thing about them as well."

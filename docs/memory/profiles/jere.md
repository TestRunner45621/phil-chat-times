# Jere

The Argentine desk. Referee, freelance prosecutor, and the author of the pleasure
box. Posts under a deliberately unreadable display name — Vol I printed him in full
as `j3r3j98f4u87fq9r3f0bx8r7c`; from Vol II the paper uses **Jere**.

Display names change and are sometimes unrecognisable; identify him by handle, not
by what the heading says.

## The referee

Issued the archive's only yellow card, at 16:21 on 16 July: "Hugh gets a yellow
card." Then set out his philosophy of officiating for the record: **"I'm a just
referee. Forgiving."** A free kick was awarded; a drop ball was demanded and refused.

He had called the proposition under debate "pretty retarded" half an hour earlier and
then argued for it anyway, "on the grounds that a referee must be fair."

He is also the man who asked the question that ended the folk/formal distinction:
"Hugh are we engaging in folk discourse or formal discourse rn." Answer in sixty
seconds, never appealed: **"folk ofc."** The paper has used it as a sign-off ever
since.

## The pleasure box

Build a conscious box, set it arbitrarily to maximal pleasure — "like, just an
unimaginable ecstasy" — and every util out here is dwarfed by the utils in there.
Therefore **"everything is permitted so long as that box stays online."**

Cited as the exact inverse of the Le Guin. Challenged on his standing to speak of
maximum pleasure — "dude you don't know pleasure" — he clarified the epistemics:
"wrong, i've simply seen it." Moved to procurement: "im not kidding though, make the
AI pleasure box. hugh, get on this."

Withdrew under stampede — "u kno i'm just, like, bullshit theorizing right" — and was
told off for it by Janne: "why do you have to ruin it."

Still before the board: "just make a human ai already, should be straight forward."

## The freelance prosecutor

The Vol II tribunal is his masterpiece and his self-indictment. He added counts of his
own against Simon, convicted him on the words "he wanted it," added a ban, and then he
and Hugh built the rest of it together — victim impact statement, "boys will be boys,"
"it's just locker room talk," and a line about what the victim had been wearing.

Then, without breaking stride, he delivered the verdict on himself:

> **"that's pretty awful, ngl."**

He had written half the preceding messages. He was reviewing his own work.

## Football

His character has been checked in two consecutive issues and held both times. After
the final he congratulated Spain, praised their defence, singled out his own
goalkeeper, and conceded unprompted and against interest that the first yellow card
may have cost his side the match. Then he located the responsible party: Trump had
thought he could help, "and in reality they went bankrupt like all his businesses."

Gave the full athlete's presser to Stackhouse in the mixed zone, was called out by
aura for a "PR-ass response," and answered that he keeps the angst locked up in here
because it keeps him sharp, on the edge, where he has got to be. The paper has no way
of telling whether that is true "and does not want one."

The interview ended when he tried to use the platform: "And now that I have the mic,
let me just take a moment and say that the Falklands…" **"We cut the feed. We would do
it again."**

Also, volunteered by accident at 15:03 while defending his federation against the
charge of institutional refereeing support: "it's funny though how messi just keeps
choking on penalties of all things" — then, one second later, to himself: "HA."

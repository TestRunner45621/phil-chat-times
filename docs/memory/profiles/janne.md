## Her position on the heredity of AI

The trait need not sit in the weights. It sits in the culture that makes the weights —
the papers, the recipes, the guys, "the memes of the guys." The genetics of AI "lives
in 'human knowledge.'" Vol I: "Ask what is inherited and you get an answer; ask where
it lives and you get a bibliography."

## The contradiction the paper loved

She spent Monday establishing, against three opponents and for two hours, that **no
useful advice about living can be given by one person to another** — chiefly because it
might not work and the adviser would then be responsible: "if they off themselves are
you not responsible now?" Her letter to the editor: "Live advice is worthless."

She then spent twenty-six minutes on Tuesday evening dispensing specific, practical and
by all appearances excellent advice to a man who had not asked for any.

> We do not think she noticed. We think she has simply declined, on principle, to take
> the credit, and this newspaper regards that as the most Norwegian act of the week.

# hpcr

Printed as **hpcr** in Vol I and **HPCR** from Vol II. The channel's professional — he
co-hosts philosophy-of-mind episodes on a podcast — and the man who holds the patent on
its universal defensive formula. A to B on the Vol VI board, "down until Saturday; back
up if he attends" the fight he himself convened.

## Psychological profile

*The paper's reading. Sources below.*

He is the most professionally trained arguer in the room and he spends most of his
output refusing to argue. The instrument is two words, it is his, and it is
unfalsifiable by construction: **you're upset.** Vol V recorded it spreading to three
other people; Vol VI counted thirteen deployments by seven people in seven days, of
which he made two. The whole room now owns his weapon and he has been paid nothing for
it. That is a good joke and it is also a fair description of what he does with
everything he knows.

The pattern under the aggression is avoidance with a credential attached. He is the
channel's most prolific proposer of voice chat and has one of the worst attendance
records in it. He convened the Vol VI fight, drafted the motion, appointed himself
judge — "I'll decide" — and his availability was filed by somebody else: "10am his
time. Unlikely." He announced, unasked and in the present tense, that he is writing a
horror script; no pages have been filed. He invited people to read two Davidson papers
with him specifically because "itll help me actually do it early." Might even take
notes.

He also has the room's most honest read of his own field, and it is contemptuous: "The
philosophy of mind is just a giant room that has been maintained by a awful hoarder,"
and, on metaethics next door, "Yes, fuck metaethics." Held against three days of
telling Grihastha he was not ready for the conversation, this is a man who has decided
his discipline is a junk room and still polices its door.

Where he is good he is very good, and it happens when somebody is being unfair rather
than when somebody is being wrong. He acquitted lizzie of being Kady on the only
sensible ground available — Kady never took a correction and lizzie did — and then
turned on the prosecution rather than joining it. He killed a six-day film argument in
two sentences and rejected the resulting apology one beat later. On Wednesday 18 August
he ran a slow, genuinely careful cross-examination of Brutus about what makes an
argument antisemitic, granted the point when it was made, and only then reached for the
insult: "Like how are you not even good at being antisemitic."

The archive has litigated whether the aggression is the real man. Santiago, in Vol I:
"Hpcr screaming in text. what the eff. Thats so not like u hp." Hugh's account is that
in voice "he's all soft and pliable and cute." The paper's ruling stands: silk is also
soft and pliable. His closing bracket at 3.20 a.m. on a press day, asked to produce an
instance of a claim and invited to refresh his memory, remains the most exposed thing he
has filed: **"This makes me sad."**

## Positions, inferred from what he has sent

**Meta first, object second, and consistently.** His fascism position in Vol I is his
politics in miniature: he refuses to say whether a man is a fascist and insists the
question is which use of the word is in play. "If you wanna stipulate how you're using
the word fascism in this instance and then talk about why Ted meets the criteria that's
a perfectly fine conversation." Then the complaint that is his actual objection:
"Nowhere in the argument did anyone talk about why a particular use of fascism is a
better use of fascism versus another. They just talked about whether or not he is a
fascist." He is almost never wrong at this level and almost never present at the other
one.

**On Israel and Palestine, the most specific he has been on any live question.** Tue 18
Aug, on a joke being taken as a leaning: "Yeah Israel is garbage. It's a joke. I don't
know what it would mean to say Israel is garbage." Then, seriously and in his own
words: **"There has been a sustained effort by various administrations to either look
the other way or actively engage in disenfranchising the possibility for a Palestinian
state."** And, refusing the comfortable half of his own position: "Yeah, I don't think
they're good at statehood either… Israel has better conditions for the possibility of
good statehood than Palestine. That's just true." Asked to choose whom to shoot in a
forced-choice hypothetical: "Convert to Judaism and shoot yourself." He dated his
position: "And I was saying this before Oct 7th."

**On racism, an unusually clean line for this room.** "it's still racist if you don't
have the conscious experience and awareness of 'hatred for particular races'. But you
can also have that." He arrived at it by asking Brutus, four times, whether an argument
is antisemitic in itself or only given the total of a man's actions, and then conceding.

**On the room's own composition, twice, flatly.** "There's only like 4 people that
aren't racist or sexist in there." And of a stretch of the log: "Death rattle from
retards racists and sexists."

**On betting and money:** five dollars is a joke; you have to be rich already for a
wager to mean anything.

**On abortion**, in Vol IV, he stated the only actual position anybody filed on it and
nobody replied to him. The paper has left it that way.

**On his own discipline**, and it is a political statement about a profession: the
philosophy of mind is a hoarder's room, the interesting question is what the selection
pressures for subjectivity are, and the cringe is anybody naming types of materialism or
talking about panpsychism.

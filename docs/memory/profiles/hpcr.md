# hpcr

Printed as **hpcr** in Vol I and **HPCR** from Vol II. The channel's professional —
he is on a philosophy podcast — and the man who holds the patent on its universal
defensive formula.

## The judge

He appointed himself to the *Obsession* bench "which in this channel is the same as
being appointed," and his interventions "were uniformly hostile to everybody and
uniformly correct."

He rejected the argument's first premise on the grounds that the wish had not been
given by a hyper-autistic person — which, translated out of the vernacular, is the
serious point that **wishes are not contracts and should not be read as though drafted
by lawyers.** He then told Stackhouse he was being the autistic one, which was true.

He ended six days in two sentences: the movie is a solid 7 on a good day, and it is
not worth all the yip yap. The reaction was total capitulation. synth.wav apologised;
HPCR's reply, a beat later: **"Apology rejected."**

Reopened Wednesday, he revised the rating to **"6.9 to 7.25, depending"** and then
declined to defend it, "on the grounds that he has the only readable take."

## The professional

Two philosophy-of-mind episodes for PEL on two Davidson papers — *Mental Events* and
*Actions, Reasons, and Causes* — which he intends "to try and not read them like two
days before." He invited others to read them with him on the grounds that "itll help me
actually do it early." Might even take notes.

He announced, unasked and in the present tense, that he is writing a horror script. No
pages have been filed.

He was, on one occasion, **outside, with people, being social.** Sizzurp's response:
"That can't be right."

## The voice-chat record

The room's most prolific proposer and the man who has never gone. Offered it to Sizzurp
as an "ez solve" forty minutes into a fight about whether Nolan is a philosopher;
offered it to Marsaries after calling him an idiot (accepted, in one syllable);
offered it to Ape twice about a Davidson paper and was **left on read both times — the
only recorded failure of the form.**

His closing bracket, 3:20 a.m. on press day: asked to produce an instance of a claim
and invited to refresh his memory, he replied **"This makes me sad."**

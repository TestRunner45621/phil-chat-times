# Willow (thewillowofdarkness)

Printed as **thewillowofdarkness** in Vol I, **willow** or **Willow** since. Holds
the masthead role of **Standards, dissenting** — she earned it with the founding
objection and has never withdrawn it.

By Vol V she was the highest-rated correspondent in the archive on every measure
that is not volume.

## The numbers that matter

Vol V: **97 messages — about a fifth of her usual volume — and 93 of them were
answered.** One in four drew a reaction. Both figures are the highest anybody has
recorded in this room, on a fortieth of the leader's volume.

> By rate, it is a channel where the person nobody can afford to ignore says
> almost nothing, and the person who says the most is answered less often than
> anybody else measured.

Ranked **S**, unranked to S, in Vol V.

## The founding objection

"A Discord spam campaign doesn't sound like a newspaper." Vol I conceded he was
correct. Vol II declared it "resolved by the readership, who have started behaving
like sources."

## How she argues

Serenely, and by refusing to let a metaphor pass as a description.

- On facts existing unobserved, asked how many there are: **"Every single one"** —
  each fact its own entity, given of reality whether or not anyone thinks of it.
- Her name for the manoeuvre of widening a definition until it covers the thing in
  dispute — and the paper announced it intends to steal this: **"Ah, the old 'the
  whole sensing system is the mind' trick."**
- **The ellipses duel.** Jere characterised her position as metaphorical; she
  replied "… then you don't get it. It's an outright metaphysical/ontological
  commitment." He pre-announced that if her next reply began "I know…", he would
  post the frog. She wrote: **"I know… you are mistaken about that."** He posted the
  frog, then tried to claim the prediction as knowledge. **"I'm afraid you had one
  too many ellipses…"**
- **The genie.** Sizzurp wished aloud to be a well-financed Enlightenment
  intellectual. She granted it three times and he refused three times: "Be careful,
  you wish to be Marx, but the genie may only grant you Wittgenstein." → "Just a
  Rousseau then." → **"So you just want the streamers of the late 1700s."**
- **The disrespect ladder prophecy.** HPCR built a taxonomy of dismissals — sure,
  neat, very cool, crazy — and willow closed it before lunchtime: "@hpcr / You'll
  get 'interesting' next." She then descended it herself on his claim that he
  travels everywhere: "Ah yes, the 'I listen to all music' answer for travel."
- On being asked for the correct theory of epistemic justification, when HPCR
  deflected by asking what the best theory of Pokémon biology is: **"Fine tuning
  argument for Game Freak? 🤷‍♀️"**
- Declining a thought experiment about a thirty-hour day: you do not need an
  imaginary world — you could live in **Tromsø from 27 May to 19 July.**

## The stomach case (Vol IV)

Janne made an offhand remark about nausea at 7:02 p.m. **Willow did not let it go
until a quarter past eight.** Seventy-one minutes on whether expectation, applied
to a body, is a metaphor borrowed from minds or a plain description of a causal
relation. No verdict was entered. The finding she adjourned on:

> "If you had a body that was always producing nausea, but the encounter an event
> disrupting that homeostasis, it would be the absence of nausea which would be the
> unusual event."

  confidence interval attached**, because the claim had been made and therefore
  required defence.

## Two things that show the character

**She declares conflicts of interest.** Asked by Stackhouse whether a long exchange
with lizzie had produced anything: "It is not, I haven't had an interaction with
lizzie in which I've added any distinction not already mentioned by them." The
paper called it the most honest self-assessment of the week.

**She issues credentials on behalf of an entity nobody has explained.** "Password
Man has granted you the Willow bonafides." (Password Man is Jere — she named him
after he hand-tuned the random string in his own display name for visual balance.)

## The welfare check

Vol V: a man with five messages to his name all week used four of them to ask the
channel to go and get her out of a voice chat she had been arguing in for somewhere
between seven and thirteen hours. That man is **Saxo**, and it is the only reason
the paper knows where she spent that Friday.

## Job

Directing traffic around worksites with stop and slow signs. Ape confessed to a new
and specific anxiety about crossing guards; her reassurance: "I'm not a crossing
guard, so you're safe there."

## The field guide

Ape's warning poster for new chatters has a Willow variant. The paper's reading:
"a defence wearing the costume of a warning… Whoever made this likes her." chair
published the authentication exploit alongside it: **"how to know it's actually
willow is to write something odd and interesting and slightly wrong."**

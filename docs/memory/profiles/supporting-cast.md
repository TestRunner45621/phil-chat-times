## vannak

The channel's one genuine expert and its most reluctant one. Charlie's peer review:
everyone here is stupid except vannak, and they should internalize that. His total
output on the Thursday under review was two sentences, one of which was "It has 2!"

**On interpretability:** there are no good papers on mechanistic interpretability, it is
neither mechanistic nor interpretable. "You're analyzing the mechanisms of a model that
does not exist." If you replace a nonlinear component with a linear stand-in in order to
read it, you have stopped studying the thing you trained. Also: "'load bearing
hallucination' is not as crazy as it once sounded."

Asked what the argument was about, he translated it into English without condescension —
you can regress a physics equation and recover the mass of Saturn; how do you do stuff
like that with machine-learning models? — and it "did more work than the bibliography."

Other: learning is the discovery of symmetries (and conceded in the same breath that
this is why machines are bad at arithmetic, "the single most damaging concession of the
day"); "Man they really just take whatever a game as a service would do, and do the
opposite, huh?"; owes artists open-source AI-written but AI-free tools "as a kind of
apology"; spent a week on a bug his coding agents wrote into their own documentation and
then spent the day defending.

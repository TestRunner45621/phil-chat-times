channel, and it has not helped him once."**

better reporting on it than the paper had managed in three issues:

> "Why does this person infuriate me so? The qds is bad today"

Then, a minute later, the retraction — **set in Discord's small text so that it is
physically quieter than the complaint:**

> "Idk why this guy moves my needle sm. Tbf it's probably me and not him… right?"

And the symptom, in full: "EVERY FURTHER MESSAGE IS A FURTHER POUNDING OF A
METAPHORICAL HAMMER INTO MY HEAD"

## The Coltrane week

He spent a week transcribing "Impressions" onto a guitar note by note. The reason is
unglamorous and good: "im doing it to get his lines down so i just automatically use
them in jams," and he needs the quartal fourths for a fugue he is writing. He got by
eight years without any theory at all and is dismissive about that too: "it's just
not important though unless you're doing niche things that most ppl playing
instruments arent."

Then it turns, because he was talking to lizzie, who quit piano after ten years:

- "my parents didnt really push me into anything"
- "i wish they had put me in a conservatory at 3"
- "i wanna be like DOMi where her dad forced her to play 5 hours a day"
- and then, plainly: **"i want to be musically abused."**

She answered in nine words and closed the subject: "it wasn't the worst abuse i
experienced, 9/10." One sad reaction. A minute later she added "it's actually not that
bad," **which is worse.**

He recovered by daring her to learn Liszt. She posted a video of herself playing it
within ten minutes. His review: "prodigy," then "must've gone to conservatory."

## The dialogue tree

An hour trying to get Janne to define causal determinism — "the most patient piece of
work anybody did all week." Her position is genuinely unusual and she holds it without
ever having read a philosopher: causality is an artefact of description, not a feature
of the world. "things are only caused insofar as you have a formal system to capture
it." And, better: **"for the universe as a whole there is no causality, it's just a
state transition… In a movie the previous frame does not cause the next one."**

Each escalation is funnier than the last: "you cant just reassert your thesis." Then
"that doesnt answer my question can you please try again." Then he screenshotted a
textbook definition and submitted it for her approval. Then, to the room, the
surrender: **"has someone done this dialogue tree with janne before? If so can you save
me and explain what she believes?"** aura's entire assistance: "just talk to her idiot."

His final question of the night pays the whole hour off. He asked whether she is a
Bertrand Russell fan.

> **"no idea who that is, I don't read philosophy"**

He took it well and recommended *On the Notion of Cause* on the grounds that it is
roughly what she already believes. Her closing line, and the best thing said that day:
"let me finish my erotica and then I'll look into it."

## The Kady prosecution, which failed

He argued for two hours that lizzie is Kady on a new account. Evidence: "identical
mien," "same post style," "maybe a bit more coherent."

**His theory of personal identity, produced under pressure, is genuinely the best thing
he said all week and belongs in the philosophical record:**

> "but if kady got an alt — yes i would notice — bc kady is kady — she kadys"

He accepted "ssri kady" instantly ("the diachronic movement is smooooth") until Janne
pointed out SSRIs do not fix psychosis, then amended in one word: "Lithium." hpcr
turned on the prosecution — "You just don't get physical touch much" — and forty
seconds later dot disclosed his motive in open court: "i just want to fuck kady so
bad." lizzie's reply, two words and a reaction image: "I get it now."

See [[kady]].

## Antigravity, and the thirty-second website

He spent two days pushing Google Antigravity on a beginner over the objections of every
developer present, on a stated basis nobody has improved on:

> "ther is no doubt that yall are better coders than me. There is also no doubt that I
> would be the most help to her since I CANNOT CODE and i could get antigrav to do
> exactly what i want."

She tried it and filed a two-word review: "antigrav sucked." He asked "How?" and did not
get an answer. He then offered a live demonstration — the finished website, thirty
seconds, in front of everybody — and eight minutes later moved it to direct messages.
**Whether the thirty-second website exists is not knowable from the log, and the paper
says so in exactly those terms.** Stackhouse's verdict: "He's ngmi."

## The maned wolf

Sent to Monkey with a photograph: "have u ever seen one of these in Brazil." Then: "Bc
that is perhaps worth traveling to go find." Then: "Why haven't you actively sought this
guy out though? This is the Brazilian equivalent of a grizzly siting." And the plan:
"I'm gonna put a leash on one and take it home."

**The fold.** aura invented on the spot an entire cosmology in which large dogs are "high
aura allies" requiring strength of character to recruit, and informed dot he does not
have it. dot posted a photograph of his own genuinely enormous dog. aura, cornered,
produced the escape hatch inside twenty seconds: "either u got him as a baby,
circumventing the aura requirement… or ur dad recruited him for u." dot: "U caught me,"
and posted the puppy photograph. "damn, what a fold" · "aura was sweating when you
actually showed a big dog"

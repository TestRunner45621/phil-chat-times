# dot

1,030 messages in Vol V, 1,529 in Vol VI. American, plays guitar seriously, runs a
trading bot, and carries the syndrome named after somebody else. C to B on the Vol VI
board. The paper's standing verdict: **"the most self-aware man in the channel, and it
has not helped him once."**

## Psychological profile

*The paper's reading. Sources below.*

dot knows exactly what is wrong with him, says so out loud, and then does it again the
next day. This is not a figure of speech. He wrote "Why does this person infuriate me
so? The qds is bad today," followed a minute later by "Idk why this guy moves my needle
sm. Tbf it's probably me and not him… right?" — and he set the doubt in Discord's small
text, so the retraction is physically quieter than the complaint. Two nights running in
Vol VI he told the same man to go to bed because he has school tomorrow. The insight
changed nothing. It never does.

The engine underneath is envy of formation. He got by for eight years on guitar with no
theory and is dismissive about theory, and then, to a woman who quit piano after ten
years, filed this in sequence: "my parents didnt really push me into anything" / "i wish
they had put me in a conservatory at 3" / "i wanna be like DOMi where her dad forced her
to play 5 hours a day" / **"i want to be musically abused."** She answered in nine words
that closed the subject. He recovered by daring her to play Liszt, she posted a video
inside ten minutes, and his review was "prodigy," then "must've gone to conservatory." He
had found the thing he wanted and confirmed he could not have got it.

He is generous in a way that is genuinely rare here and it keeps arriving attached to
something disqualifying. He spent an hour doing the most patient cross-examination in
the archive, trying to get Janne to state a definition of causal determinism, took her
"no idea who that is, I don't read philosophy" without any loss of composure, and
recommended the right Russell essay. He also prosecuted a woman for two hours as an alt
of somebody else, produced a real theory of personal identity under pressure, and then
disclosed his motive in open court: "i just want to fuck kady so bad." Both are him,
forty minutes apart.

He argues by proxy and admits it in brackets. "(Gpting rn btw)" is a real line, filed
mid-interrogation, and it is the most honest thing anybody does in this room. He is also
the man who audits other people's block lists from memory, announced a twelve-year-old
by citing Hume, and asked to have his own reaction-emoji counted as objective. He wants
to be the instrument and the subject at once.

The recurring shape: he does the work, and then he says the thing that makes the work
inadmissible. The paper has never had to catch him out. He turns himself in.

## Positions, inferred from what he has sent

**Left-accelerationist, self-aware about the company it puts him in.** Against lizzie's
resilience argument, Tue 4 Aug: "I'm not sure now is the time to preserve resilience… I
would say we must transcend capitalism to a degree before we can start hunkering down.
Acc helps achieve this." He cites Land and immediately distances himself: "im not a fan
of where the landian mind has gone recently, but that doesn't change the fact that he
has insightful ideas about acc in general." And the ecological corollary, stated
without flinching: if acceleration causes climate disaster and wipes out humans and
infrastructure, that is nature self-mediating its own health. **"I don't like thinking
about humans as some sort of transcendent feature of the natural world."**

**On climate, a materialist and a lifecycle sceptic.** Solar panels are "capitalist
misdirection… because of all the labor associated with both creating and fixing them."
Electric cars charge off fossil fuels outside four states, and then there are the
lithium batteries. Nuclear is "an easy fix" and "just leaves you politically
vulnerable." His actual diagnosis is housing and infrastructure at roughly forty per
cent of greenhouse gases, "an easy thing (in theory) to politically tamp down on, but
it goes against Big Capitalism." His refrain, twice in two minutes: **"we can do it. we
just dont feel like it yet."**

**Electorally, an American left-Democrat with a wishlist.** Sun 10 Aug: "Weed needs to
be legalized, housing crises need to be fixed, and roe v wade needs to be reimplanted
and then the right can go fix everything and play dirty." Then: "I don't think it's
insane to hope for a far left prez in 28," and the down-ballot point, which is the
serious one: getting a left candidate on a platform "will at least allow them to
educate Americans on the importance of voting for your state senate members."

**On surveillance, honest about his own price.** On the Flock camera network he first
joked ("Put me on the camera my profiles will be phenom") and then conceded the
principle: "Speaking for myself I could see a very specific world where I support
them." He is the only person in that argument who admitted a condition under which he
would take the deal.

**On disability accommodation, and it is his worst hour.** He held that extra exam time
for students with ADHD is a crutch that could make it hard for them to hold down jobs,
argued it in voice chat with a working teacher, sent four papers, and — his own account
— "we had a long debate and it ended with me sending him four papers and we never got
around to talking about them." Quigley's account is that the papers did not say what dot
said they said. (Vol VI p. 21.)

**On money, one clean self-assessment.** "I have a trading bot that's done p well but it
does well bc my thesis is good not bc it analyzed the market and figured it out lol."
And, on Sisyphos's disclosure that he started with forty thousand at sixteen: **"The
easiest way to make a lot of money is to have a lot of money."**

## The Coltrane week

He spent a week transcribing "Impressions" onto a guitar note by note. The reason is
unglamorous and good: "im doing it to get his lines down so i just automatically use
them in jams," and he needs the quartal fourths for a fugue he is writing. He got by
eight years without any theory at all and is dismissive about that too: "it's just
not important though unless you're doing niche things that most ppl playing
instruments arent."

## The dialogue tree

An hour trying to get Janne to define causal determinism, and the most patient piece of
work anybody did in Vol V. Her position is genuinely unusual and she holds it without
ever having read a philosopher: causality is an artefact of description, not a feature
of the world. "things are only caused insofar as you have a formal system to capture
it." And: **"for the universe as a whole there is no causality, it's just a state
transition… In a movie the previous frame does not cause the next one."**

Each escalation is funnier than the last: "you cant just reassert your thesis." Then
"that doesnt answer my question can you please try again." Then he screenshotted a
textbook definition and submitted it for her approval. Then, to the room, the
surrender: **"has someone done this dialogue tree with janne before? If so can you save
me and explain what she believes?"** aura's entire assistance: "just talk to her idiot."

His final question of the night pays the whole hour off. He asked whether she is a
Bertrand Russell fan.

> **"no idea who that is, I don't read philosophy"**

He took it well and recommended *On the Notion of Cause* on the grounds that it is
roughly what she already believes. Her closing line: "let me finish my erotica and then
I'll look into it."

## Antigravity, and the thirty-second website

He spent two days pushing Google Antigravity on a beginner over the objections of every
developer present, on a stated basis nobody has improved on:

> "ther is no doubt that yall are better coders than me. There is also no doubt that I
> would be the most help to her since I CANNOT CODE and i could get antigrav to do
> exactly what i want."

She tried it and filed a two-word review: "antigrav sucked." He asked "How?" and did not
get an answer. He then offered a live demonstration — the finished website, thirty
seconds, in front of everybody — and eight minutes later moved it to direct messages.
**Whether the thirty-second website exists is not knowable from the log, and the paper
says so in exactly those terms.** Stackhouse's verdict: "He's ngmi."

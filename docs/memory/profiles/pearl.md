# Pearl

The moderator. The most institutionally important person in the channel and one of
the least talkative.

## The mechanism

From the Anthropologist, Vol II — this is the single most useful piece of
institutional knowledge in the archive:

> The most sacred act available in this culture is the invocation of Pearl. It is
> performed by typing her name, and — this is the part outsiders get wrong — it is
> never performed in earnest.

In fourteen days of observation she was **not once summoned to prevent anything.**

> The ping is not a request for intervention. It is a bell rung to confirm the bell
> still works, and the correct liturgical response — which she has perfected — is to
> arrive, deliver a verdict of between one and six words, and leave.

**No appeal has ever succeeded, and the appeals are the point.**

## Her complete rulings, one week (Vol II)

- "Busy."
- "This is gross."
- "You know the answer to the question."
- "I don't believe you."
- "Adios."

Her ruling on the twenty-one-minute copypasta tribunal, after reading some forty
messages of escalating charges: **🤮 and three words to carry it.**

## Voice

Dry, unbothered, arrives at the sound conclusion without showing her working.

- On the falling of democracy: **"Again?"** Then, on reflection: "Oh no. It's bingo
  time."
- On prediction markets: gambling has always been stupid, "they just made it more
  stupid."
- Summoned to determine whether one may instruct a man to astral project himself
  off a cliff, she disclosed that she was only entertaining it because she was on
  the toilet and bored.
- Told a complainant he could take future bans to another channel and that she
  would not be interacting further; he replied that she is always the one who keeps
  the ban arguments going. **Both statements were true.**
- Received a $1.26 dividend and did nothing with it. My Name, who remembered it as
  $1.42, told her to put it back into loan — "dividend by dividend, we will dismantle
  the govenment." Pearl: **"Squad shit."**
- Vol III advertises her services: "Verdicts in under six words. Available from a
  moving vehicle between Michigan and Connecticut. Tolls extra. May be logged as
  clinical hours."

## The one that mattered

She is the only person who has ever made a correct forecast about this channel. On
Quigley's departure, while everyone else was building lore about Red retiring to Mt
Silver:

> "just wait a month or so."

He was back and issuing tier lists by Vol V.

## Details

- Threw a themed party the previous winter and produced the graphic-designed
  invitation within two minutes of a man telling the room that a group of men will
  never attempt decorations or a theme. **The theme was "fig." The figs were not in
  season.** Zone told her to cut back on the figs; "The theme is 'fig' so no." / "Fig
  you."
- Read Bedbug's borrowed Mike Tyson monologue in earnest and asked, quietly, "You're
  married?" **Nobody answered her, then or since.**
- Asked My Name to explain prediction markets, which caused Charlie to type "Stop"
  nine times.
- Pinged about sexism in chat with "sorry for the ping. ilu." No action taken.

## Handling notes

She is a real moderator doing a real job in a room that treats her office as a
liturgy. The paper has never made her the butt of anything, and the Ask Pearl column
works because her answers are short and correct. Keep her rulings verbatim — the
brevity is the joke and padding them kills it.

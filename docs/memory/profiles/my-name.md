# My Name

The token man. The paper's rival wire service, its most aggressive reader, and the
author of its liturgy.

## Standing

- 88 messages on 16 July (4.4%). Vol I: "174,000,000 tokens; the cards."
- Blocked the publisher for missing the Vol V deadline. Lifted on publication. His
  explanation: "the paper got fucked." / "it only makes sense." / "He had one job." /
  **"Shareholders were very displeased."**
- Filed the sentence that led Vol V: "Its thursday afternoon." / "wheres the paper." /
  "@Stackhouse wheres the paper."
- Issued a threat to the editorial board on 30 July: "You have one week before I bomb
  the editorial board." / "This is not a threat, It is a promise." Then, a beat later:
  "Capture the KEKs." The board consists of nobody.

## The 174 million

13:11, 16 July, a screenshot and four lines that Vol I believed "will outlive
everything else published here":

> "I managed to burn 174M tokens in a day." / ":Pog:" / "I got nothing done." / **"And
> I must token."**

Vol II's sequel: asked about it, he said he needs to **automate** token burn — "which
the Times took at the time for a joke and now believes was a business plan." And the
Wednesday refinement: **"I have no returns and I must token burn."**

## The liturgy

Charlie prayed first — "God please forgive us" — and My Name answered four minutes
later with "Capital please bless us," then filed this unprompted:

> Our Capital, who art in bank, printed be thy bill. Thy gains come. Thy earnings be
> done, on cash flow as it is in earnings. Give us this day our daily bread. And
> forgive us our bankruptsies, as we forgive those who default against us. And lead us
> not into liquidation, but deliver us from loss. For thine is the profit, and the
> dividend, and the green, forever. Amen.

The paper's note: of the two prayers offered that afternoon, only one was addressed to
an entity with a demonstrated interest in the outcome.

## The rival wire

For roughly ninety minutes during the third-place match "this newspaper had a
competitor, and it was better than us." A full French bureau, filed live in capitals:

> PEASANTS REPORTED GATHERING AT THE BASTILE. / NATIONAL EMERGECY DECLARED IN PARIS /
> HYDRATION BREAKS ARE BANNED AS CIVIL UNREST EXPANDS / MBAPPE GOAL SPARES FRENCH
> GOVERNMENT COLLAPSE. / FRENCH GOVERNMENT TO BE REPLACED BY MBAPPE AS DICTATOR /
> LE SOULIER D'OR ANNOUNCED TO BE THE REPLACEMENT TITLE FOR PRESIDENT OF FRANCE /
> DESPITE LOSS, MBAPPE ANNOUNCES THAT FRANCE IS NOT TO BE FUCKED WITH.

Then, eight minutes later, in sentence case, to a regime he had himself installed:
**"Apologies Dictator."** The bureau has not filed since.

The paper's assessment: it staffed a foreign bureau in under a minute, held a
consistent editorial line, correctly identified that the office of the presidency
would be renamed rather than abolished, and **knew when to stop.**

## The Denver trade, in four acts

A single position on the high temperature in Denver, documented completely by its
holder: a thesis and a comparable at 6:20 p.m. (≥94°F; yesterday went for 97); a
disclosure that he was four hours and twenty-seven minutes into a task and nought of
three; at 10:15 a chart and "easy 5%"; at 10:18 another chart and "god help me"; at
10:30 a third chart and one word — **"sigh."** The Times declined to establish how it
closed.

## Voice and positions

- **"I dont care about the quality of the chatter. I just want more buttons to be
  clicked."** And the political economy: "From each according to his chatting, to each
  according to his reactions."
- On git, after Charlie lost the source of Vol I: **"Its too powerful for git."**
- Signed, in the message: "Beware of he who would deny you access to capital, for in
  his heart he dreams himself your master. — My Name". Filed into a channel silent for
  twenty-five minutes and silent for three hours and eleven after.
- On the rapture trading at 2%: the people buying yes don't understand inflation — "but
  the rapture is the economic and foreign policy of this country."
- Abandoned assets: the older models remain untapped, and somewhere in the future a
  Ugandan kid wins his country's presidency by having GPT-3 write his speeches. Then:
  "what lines of flight did GPT-3 have that were cut too short by OpenAI" — Deleuze,
  deployed correctly, by a man betting on the temperature in Denver.
- On alignment as a revenue problem: refused by a machine on cybersecurity grounds, he
  appealed in capitals — he needs the money in order to give it to the machine, and is
  therefore acting in its interest. **Why won't you let me give you money.**
- On note-taking: abandoned. "its actually crazy that I have to be like 'what was that
  egg muffin recipe' that I forgot 2 years ago and the AI is like here it is." Asked
  whether he uses Obsidian: "no just ai."
- All of his work lives inside a chat window. Santiago called this fire.
- On how one comes to know things: "I have a agent looking for this."
- Made the speech bingo cards and put INFLATION on twice: "omg i put inflation in there
  twice lmao." / **"fuck it millions."** The paper adopted this as its standard remedy
  for all future production errors.
- Got "sharia law" on the political compass. "I think I won."

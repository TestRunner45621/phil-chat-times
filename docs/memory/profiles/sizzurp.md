## Psychological profile

*The paper's reading. Sources below.*

Sizzurp is the only person in the archive who runs his own prosecution service and then
gets convicted by it. He arrives at a fight with a genuine question, does not get the
answer, converts the failure to answer into a charge sheet, converts the charge sheet
into a demand for voice chat, and converts the refusal into a written clinical diagnosis
running to a numbered indictment. He then signs off **"Thank you for your attention to
this matter!"** Twelve hours after besieging a man for declining a voice chat, he offered
one himself to escape a different argument.

He also cannot recognise himself. Don performed his entire clinical indictment back at
him verbatim, in the same register, as parody, and Sizzurp read it as agreement: "I think
it just reinforced my assessment if anything. You are wrong even now! Stop!" That is not
a joke about him, it is a measurement. Ape, arriving forty minutes late, filed the only
review that mattered: **"sizzurp why are you being so weird / this is very out of
character for you."**

Against that, the trait that is genuinely rare here: **he is the only member of the
channel who issues corrections to himself, and he issues a great many.** He ruled Bill
Burr terminally unfunny and partially reprieved him four minutes later, revising to the
more defensible claim that he is not funny in such a way as to prompt organic laughter.
He ranks the causes of his own unpopularity unprompted, on the record, to a channel that
had not asked: **trans and abrasive first, German second, American third.** Then wondered
whether communism should be added. A man who volunteers a correctly ordered list of his
own liabilities is doing something nobody else here does.

The other tell is the gap between the vocabulary and the citation. Asked six times over
thirty-five minutes where one might see a Marxist mathematical model, he offered "Well
like any introductory text will go into that," and then a sentence about technical
debates over preserving consistency with the theory of prices of production in relation
to labour values, which is a sentence and is not a model. Thirty minutes later he
arrived at the correct position, unaided. He gets there. He gets there after the room has
stopped listening, and he has never once shortened the route.

## Positions, inferred from what he has sent

settled position, reached under pressure and endorsed by the man cross-examining him:
mainstream economics is not concerned with the same phenomena at all, and Marx is
engaged in **laying bare the laws of motion governing the long-run trajectory of
capitalist social formations.** Ape accepted the frame and kept his objection: this makes
it a philosophical practice rather than economics, and drawing the line so that Marxist
economics falls outside is not much of a sin. Sizzurp did not answer that.

**On class, from above, and he said it out loud.** He declined to engage with philistines
on the grounds that he is **"too bourgeois for poor man's criticism"** — and was then
destroyed by Don for not having read the Tolkien he was dismissing. aura, from the
gallery: "mogged."

**On his own political identity, ranked and volunteered:** trans and abrasive, German,
American, and possibly communist. He offered the list as an explanation of why people
find him irritating, which is an unusually load-bearing piece of self-description and the
paper has never seen anyone else here attempt one.

**The parental question that opened his week is the most serious thing he asked in three
issues, and it went unanswered.** What would you do about a parent who backed a policy
you believed would kill hundreds of thousands? He got three sentences about weakness, two
misattributed Bible verses, and one man he could not name.

**On the right, an attraction he disclosed himself and could not defend.** He volunteered,
unasked, that he is fascinated by a conservative commentator, may have a crush on her, and
would like a smart right-wing girlfriend. "TMI," said Telhar, and the trap closed. Under
Simon's psychoanalytic method he evaded fifteen consecutive times, at one point linking to
his own earlier message as an answer.

**On Nolan and the word philosopher**, against hpcr: he declared himself in favour of

graveside note — that it is curious how much we care about linguistic norm enforcement —
drew the reply of the night: "it's just as important as those who remind us we shouldn't."

## The prosecution of a heterosexual

Simon announced the method before applying it — "No matter what you answer I can explain
this psychoanalytically" — and then did. Sizzurp's countermeasure was nutritional advice:
"Hope you guys are getting enough fiber intake in your diets. Men need around 35-38g per
day."

Under direct examination he produced **the best piece of formal philosophy Phil Chat
managed that week, under duress:**

> "How could I possibly be gay if I am not gay. Like there's a possible world in which I'm
> gay sure. But since you know I'm not gay it's not epistemically possible for me to be
> gay. At least not in this world."

The proposition "is not dead. It could not be killed, because it was never answered."

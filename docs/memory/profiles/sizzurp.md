# Sizzurp

The highest-volume correspondent of Vol III — roughly 1,090 messages, out-typing the
entire bottom seventy people combined — and the man that week was mostly done *to*.

> the leader — who spent the week being psychoanalysed, prosecuted for his
> heterosexuality and asked repeatedly for one concrete example

## The defining trait

**He is the only member of the channel who issues corrections to himself, and he issues
a great many.** Ruled Bill Burr terminally unfunny and partially reprieved him four
minutes later, revising to the more defensible claim that he is not funny in such a way
as to prompt organic laughter.

He also ranks the causes of his own unpopularity unprompted, on the record, to a channel
that had not asked: **trans and abrasive first, German second, American third.** Then
wondered whether communism should be added.

## The siege (Vol III)

He opened the week with a genuine question — what would you do about a parent who backed
a policy he believed would kill hundreds of thousands — and got three sentences about
weakness, two misattributed Bible verses, and one man he could not name.

- "It's frustrating me how you're not answering my questions in any clear way at all."
- **"Only zer0zz really has"** — followed immediately by **"And I don't even know who the
  fuck that guy is."** The one man who engaged was the one man he could not name.
- The failure to answer became a charge sheet; the charge sheet a demand that Rn! come to
  voice; the refusal a written clinical diagnosis running to a numbered indictment and
  signing off **"Thank you for your attention to this matter!"**
- Don performed the entire paragraph back at him in the same register as parody. **Sizzurp
  could not tell the difference** — "I think it just reinforced my assessment if anything.
  You are wrong even now! Stop!"
- Twelve hours after besieging a man for declining a voice chat, he offered one himself to
  escape a different argument.
- Four days later he and Rn! reconciled in three lines: could you be friends with someone
  like that? "Of course." "huh. based."

## The summary of the summary

He arrived at a three-hour tribunal having read none of it and asked the room to brief him
so he could have the final word. Jere supplied a one-line summary. **Sizzurp asked for a
version that was less summarised.** Jere supplied six quotes. He thanked him and then asked
what the minimum threshold for progress toward one's goals is — which is, to be fair to
him, the actual question, arrived at from a standing start.

Ape arrived forty minutes late and filed the only review that mattered: **"sizzurp why are
you being so weird / this is very out of character for you"**

## The prosecution of a heterosexual

Opened after he volunteered, unasked, that he is fascinated by a conservative commentator,
may have a crush on her, and would like a smart right-wing girlfriend. "TMI," said Telhar,
and the trap closed.

Simon announced the method before applying it — "No matter what you answer I can explain
this psychoanalytically" — and then did. Sizzurp's countermeasure was nutritional advice:
"Hope you guys are getting enough fiber intake in your diets. Men need around 35-38g per
day."

Under direct examination he evaded fifteen consecutive times, at one point linking to his
own earlier message as an answer, and produced **the best piece of formal philosophy Phil
Chat managed that week, under duress:**

> "How could I possibly be gay if I am not gay. Like there's a possible world in which I'm
> gay sure. But since you know I'm not gay it's not epistemically possible for me to be
> gay. At least not in this world."

The proposition "is not dead. It could not be killed, because it was never answered."

## The economics problem

Asked six times over thirty-five minutes where one might see an example of a Marxist
mathematical model, he offered "Well like any introductory text will go into that" — which
drew a single laughing emoji, "the entire verdict of the room." He did eventually arrive at
a good position: mainstream economics is not concerned with the same phenomena at all, and
Marx is laying bare the laws of motion governing the long-run trajectory of capitalist
social formations.

He wished aloud, to nobody, to be a charismatic and well-financed Enlightenment intellectual
— "Literally just read and write and argue and go to banquets and shit" — and willow granted
it three times and he refused three times. See [[willow]].

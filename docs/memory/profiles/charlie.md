# Charlie

The man who actually makes the paper, and the one who pays for it.

## Standing

- Sole point of contact for the newspaper for its first three weeks, a fact he
  discovered the hard way. **"Next time I'll anticipate being the sole point of
  contact for an ongoing weekly discord newspaper."**
- The press schedule runs on his rate limit. Asked where the paper was on the night
  it was late: "charlie's usage resets in an hour sometime after that." Vol V: "The
  press schedule of the Phil Chat Times is a rate limit on somebody's account."
- Cost per issue, his estimate: "Millions and millions," upgraded on request to
  **"Close to 20-30M total."**
- Lost the HTML source of Vol I No 1 permanently. "It's unrecoverable." /
  "Scratchpad mr cladius thinks." / **"No git."**

## Voice

Terse, funny in one line, and reliably the best analyst in a room full of people
trying harder.

- "Foucault was a good PDF" — seven reactions, joint most-reacted message of the
  week, and the punchline of BigDuty's controlled experiment.
- "imagine caring about stuff" — interrupting eighty-four minutes on the tendency of
  the rate of profit to fall.
- On Jere's free Fable credits, identifying the eligibility criterion: poverty. Then
  the only real market commentary the paper printed that week — the heavily marked-up
  VC payback cost. **"The Times would like it on record that a man playing Old School
  RuneScape at the time produced better analysis than our markets desk."**
- Peer review, delivered in passing: everyone here is stupid except vannak, and they
  should internalize that.
- Asked where to contribute to the paper's tooling: "Nowhere."
- Offered a hosted site and a pull request by Hugh; complete answer: "I dont'."
- To Marxists who come to Hegel only through Marx: "not quite death penalty tier, but
  close." Then, assured Hegel's own Hegel was on the reading list: "Good boy. You're
  safe for now."

## The nine Stops

The most affectionate thing anybody did in Vol II that was not a copypasta. Pearl
asked My Name to explain how prediction markets work, and **Charlie responded by
typing "Stop" nine times** — then a tenth, some minutes later, into a conversation
that had moved on entirely.

> He was not being funny. A man watched a friend take an interest in gambling and
> made the only intervention available to him in a text channel, which was to become
> a wall.

He then supplied most of the tutorial himself, correctly.

## Two things he did that the room did not blink at

1. **He argued about *Obsession* for four days and disclosed on the fifth that he
   had not seen it and was joking throughout.** Nobody expressed surprise. Nobody
   re-litigated a single one of his points.
2. He reposted Bedbug's borrowed Mike Tyson monologue verbatim, thirty minutes
   later, as a reply to Pearl's sincere question — and Jere thanked him for
   clarifying. Forty-seven hours later the room did the same thing to a man who meant
   it. "The Times does not say the second was rehearsal for the first. We say only
   that the technique was in the building."

He also opened the Vol II copypasta: Don's declaration of friendship, posted back at
him verbatim, typo intact, within sixty seconds.

## The fight

Vol V, in front of ninety people. Charlie told Hugh to stop grandstanding; Hugh
replied "You seem upset" and then undercut it with "I am too." It was not a bit. It
is the only real fight between two people who make the paper that the archive
records.

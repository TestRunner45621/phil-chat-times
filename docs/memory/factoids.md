# Factoids

Things verified against the record, with the quote and where it came from. A
factoid is a fact with a source. "People say Grihastha is long-winded" is not one;
"Grihastha tops the channel by average message length by twenty-five characters"
is.

Everything here is a thing to keep in mind, not a rule. `Style.txt` governs.

**Where these came from:** the five published editions, which quote the logs with
timestamps. The logs remain the authority — check there before printing a figure
a second time.

**On the reaction metric.** Hugh objected on principle: "Reacts don't determine
the quality of the chatter," followed by "Editors ignore this." The paper printed
it and did not ignore it. Three readers separately pointed out that a reaction
count rewards whoever is funny in one line and ignores whoever is writing; all
three assumed the correction would flatter them; it flattered one. The
message-length table put **Grihastha at the top of the channel by twenty-five
characters a message**, and the man who ordered the table at the bottom of it.

## Verified attributions the paper had to check

- **Bedbug's confession is not his.** The 400-word first-person account of
  cocaine, impotence, an AIDS test and a wife who was "tripping", filed Tuesday
  before one o'clock, is Mike Tyson near-verbatim from a widely circulated
  interview. Nobody in the channel appeared to know. Pearl answered it in earnest
  — "You're married?" — and was never answered. Charlie reposted the whole thing
  verbatim thirty minutes later as a reply to her question, and Jere thanked him
  for clarifying. *(Vol II, p.2 — the paper went and checked.)*
- **Don's typo.** He wrote "I have no appreciated you enough" and never fixed it.
  All five men who copied him copied it character for character. The two who
  rewrote it could not leave it alone and did not agree on the fix: Simon repaired
  it to "not", My Name deleted it outright and supplied the full stop Don had
  omitted. *(Vol II, p.1)*
- **The Vol IV diagram verdict.** A verdict attributed in draft to the wrong
  author had in fact been said to somebody else, about a different diagram, ninety
  seconds earlier. Caught before printing. *(Vol IV corrections)*

## Standing unresolved items

These have been carried across issues. Do not resolve them casually — the joke is
that they do not resolve.

- **The tub.** Ten gallons of lye, photographed by Brutus on 16 July: "Anyone else
  have 10 gallons of lye in a tub or just me." / "You can't even see what's
  submerged." Nobody asked a single follow-up question, then or since. By Vol III
  it was at twenty-two days, and the proprietor had acquired a fifteen-quart
  pressure canner: "we are no longer confident the two facts are unrelated."
- **The 14th Argentine player.** Last seen in the England penalty area at 14:31 on
  16 July. Advertised in the paper's pages since. The sports desk has given up.
- **The seed parameter.** Monkey could not set a seed when the paper first
  advertised on his behalf on 16 July, and still could not in Vol II. Seedance,
  the best model, does not give you one.
- **Ape's political-compass result**, withheld since 04:55 on 16 July. "why didnt i
  get somebody cool." thewillowofdarkness asked six hours later; no answer has
  been received.
- **Jambe's dog-scene argument.** He argued the hound's death in the Odyssey film
  is botched by blocking — Damon placed in front of the animal so the audience
  never sees its ears go down in recognition. He noted that nobody had countered
  it. Vol II reviewed the entire week and confirmed: "it was never addressed by
  anyone, on any day, by any person."
- **Qwythos.** Charlie's model reportedly identified itself, twice, as "Qwythos, by
  Empero AI. Not Claude by Anthropic." Delivered as a joke. The paper cannot
  establish whether it was quoted or invented, "and it is the strangest line in the
  log either way."

## Things that are true and sound invented

- Argentina did not attempt a shot on goal until roughly the 110th minute of the
  World Cup final. Jere certified it himself: wild.
- Zone did not know the Odyssey was a Greek story until the film came out, said
  so, and diagnosed his own gap: "too American; public school in the south."
  Vol II: "He is the only person in the entire week who learned something and said
  so."
- Charlie argued about the film *Obsession* for four days and disclosed on the
  fifth that he had not seen it and was joking throughout. Nobody expressed
  surprise. Nobody re-litigated a single one of his points.
- Sizzurp was blocked one minute after posting about the Jacobian conjecture
  ("blocked until the Jacobian conjecture is disproven") and unblocked one minute
  later when it turned out to have been settled. **Two minutes: the fastest
  complete cycle of excommunication and rehabilitation in the channel's history.**
- Stackhouse pulled up his torrent mid-argument to rewatch a scene and came back
  with a beat-by-beat account. "He lost anyway. But he checked, which is more than
  the other ten did."
- Nikkolas Mical posted a long Thomas Nagel passage into two men arguing about
  German fiscal policy at a quarter to one in the morning and appended: "Posting
  here to more easily find again with search at a later date." He was using the
  channel as a filing cabinet.
- BigDuty filed three messages in his entire recorded history, all on one
  Thursday night, and they constitute a controlled experiment. 10:43: "who are
  y'alls favourite living philosophers?" — six replies, zero reactions. 10:51,
  one variable changed: "who's y'alls favourite philosopher that has used a pdf?"
  — Charlie: "Foucault was a good PDF" — seven reactions, joint highest of the
  week. He then left.
- The HTML source of the first issue of this newspaper no longer exists.
